Who is NexHire for?+

NexHire is designed primarily for college students, final-year students, and fresh graduates preparing for internships and entry-level jobs.

Does NexHire guarantee a job?+

No. NexHire is a career support tool designed to help you prepare and make informed decisions. Hiring outcomes depend on many factors.

Is NexHire connected to live job portals?+

This landing-page demo illustrates the planned experience. Live job integrations and advanced AI features would need to be connected as the product develops.

Can colleges work with NexHire?+

Yes. NexHire plans to offer college and placement-centre partnerships to support student employability and career preparation.
